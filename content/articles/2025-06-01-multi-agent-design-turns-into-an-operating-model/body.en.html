Before splitting the work, check its dependencies

Assigning separate agents to research, writing, and review does not automatically create effective collaboration. Writing depends on research, while style checking and source verification may proceed independently from the same draft. Those dependencies determine the execution pattern.

Semantic Kernel's May 2025 announcement exposed several such patterns through an SDK. The useful design question is what each stage receives and when the job is complete.

Sequential, concurrent, and handoff patterns wait for different things

The Semantic Kernel article describes sequential output passing, concurrent independent work followed by aggregation, and context-dependent handoffs. These are implementation choices, not evidence that one fits every task.

Consider a hypothetical writing workflow. Drafting follows completed research. Independent style and source checks can run together, with revision waiting for both. A specialist handoff needs another decision: does responsibility transfer, or does the original agent resume after receiving advice?

Pass evidence to the reviewer, not just conclusions

If a researcher returns only a claim that a product is fastest, the reviewer must reconstruct the comparison. Passing the source, relevant passage, measurement conditions, and unresolved questions makes verification specific. Short handoffs should not discard necessary evidence.

Revision can also continue indefinitely if reviewers keep requesting changes. Distinguish mandatory factual corrections, optional wording suggestions, and decisions requiring a person. Define termination conditions. Creating multiple roles does not itself implement stopping rules or permission separation.

Measure the benefit alongside coordination cost

Anthropic's Building effective agents recommends beginning with simpler approaches and adding complexity when needed. A single agent that drafts and reviews is a useful baseline here.

Using the same inputs, compare factual errors corrected, missing evidence, time, and usage cost. Parallel work still waits for slow branches, and incomplete handoffs can cause repeated research. Include the human correction burden in the result.

Choose a division of labor around work with definable inputs, outputs, and verification, rather than role names alone. That makes the choice of an SDK orchestration pattern explainable.
