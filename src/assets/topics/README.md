# Topic illustrations

Generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool for the topic overview. These are abstract editorial illustrations, not screenshots or evidence of a product. They are decorative (`alt=""`) and never replace article titles or source information.

- `topic-flow.webp`: information flowing into an artifact.
- `topic-execution.webp`: controlled execution and verified output.

Delivery: 960 × 640 WebP, quality 85. Original generated PNGs remain in the local generated-images directory.

## Flow prompt

Create a clean premium editorial illustration for a Japanese AI research news website. Wide landscape 3:2 composition, no text, no letters, no logo, no people, no real computer screenshot. Microsoft Future 2009 inspired quiet near-future information workspace. Three matte white thin rectangular planes float in shallow depth, one front white panel, tiny translucent seafoam glass nodes connected by one elegant pale cyan ribbon path. Very minimal scientific precision, plenty of soft ice-white negative space. White opaque surfaces, tiny frosted translucent edge details, muted mint and desaturated blue, soft natural ambient shadows. Orthographic editorial still life, abstract meaningful flow of information into an artifact. NOT neon, NOT dark sci-fi, NOT stock office photography, no decorative circuitry, no luminous rings. Composition readable as small thumbnail. High quality restrained three dimensional render, compatible with a white clean Japanese sans serif editorial UI.
